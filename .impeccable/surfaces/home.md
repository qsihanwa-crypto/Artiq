## Direction contract

THESIS: Monochrome gallery chrome disappears so artwork is the page's only color — refusing both the maximalist "colorful everywhere" template and the flat white photo-portfolio default.

OWN-WORLD: White canvas, black type, frosted glass (60–75% white, 20px blur, 8% black border) used only on nav/filters/cards. Space Grotesk display, Inter body. A thin black signature line threads every section. Art supplies every hue on the page.

STORY: Visitor reads the mission line, is pulled deeper by an infinite spiral of art, meets the artist as a person, plays "How I See," browses the full catalogue, and is invited to support him — never blocked by animation.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Home hero — full-bleed white canvas, huge left-aligned three-line black headline, supporting line and two buttons below, one floating artwork fragment with soft parallax at right, signature line drawn faintly beneath the headline.

FORM: Brief-pinned direction — the user's 60-section spec fully fixes palette, type, component grammar, and page order. Concept-seed roll skipped: a user/brief-pinned direction beats the roll, always. No seed key.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance.
